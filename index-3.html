About Us

Chez Mimi is a small café in the heart of Vienna. We roast our beans with care, bake fresh cake every day and take the time for what makes a good coffee house: a cozy spot where you want to stay a while.

Roasted with care

Small batches, big flavour. Our beans are roasted fresh every week.

Baked every day

Cakes, tarts and flaky pastries – still warm when they reach your table.

A cozy corner

Soft chairs, good music and nobody rushing you. Stay as long as you like.
